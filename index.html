Meet Mat

Why I started this.

In all honesty, I wanted to get better at listening. Better at communicating. I wanted conversations that would actually change me.

I was curious, but I also knew I needed to meet my own edges. That meant building something real, episode after episode, with no audience and no safety net. Just a system and a commitment to show up honestly.

I launched Edge Within on January 20, 2026 with zero followers. Every episode, every clip, every conversation has been built from scratch in real time. No ads. No shortcuts. Just consistency, curiosity, and the willingness to have the conversations that mattered.
