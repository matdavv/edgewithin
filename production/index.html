On site

  • Full 4K setup, lighting, premium mics. Same kit I use on Edge Within
  • Your place or a site we agree. Indoor or shaded outdoor
  • One person running the day. I interview you, with question ideas and prompts if you'd like them, or you host and I facilitate

After the shoot

What you get, every episode.

Every episode includes

  • Long-form edit with colour grading (multicam)
  • 5 short-form clips + custom thumbnail
  • Two rounds of revisions
  • Titles, descriptions, chapters
  • Upload to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube once you approve
  • You own the content

Typical turnaround: long-form review in 7-10 business days, clips within 5 business days of approval.
